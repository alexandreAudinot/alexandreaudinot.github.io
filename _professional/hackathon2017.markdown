In the first VR hackathon I ever participated in, our team decided to make a game based on Harry potter and the Goblet of Fire to fit the theme "cinema".
The goal is to navigate a maze to find the Goblet, and the player has a magic wand with a set of 4 spells they can cast: Lumos to creates light around the wand, Wingardium Leviosa to move objects around, Alohomora to open doors, and Patronus to defend against Dementors.

The twist of the game is that we used impossible spaces techniques based of this [paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/3DUI.2017.7893327) to create a seamless and natural walking experience despite being confined to roughly 2x3m.

The game also featured a Marauder's map only visible to the people not wearing the headset, showing the position of the player at all time.
