At a 4-day hackathon organized by Inria Rennes with a focus on education, we developed an interactive VR scenario to introduce users to the General Relativity theory.
The scenario begins with the player on a desolate, lifeless Earth. They soon discover a spaceship controlled by a playful AI named Doppler, and are greeted by a hologram of Albert Einstein who introduces the theory and guides the user on an interactive space and time travel experience to escape from Earth.
<br/><br/><br/>
![minigame](/img/hackathon_2019/Screenshot_2022-10-19_15-15-16_1920x1080.png "One of the minigames"){: .align-left}
Throughout the journey, users engage in 2 mini-games: one in which they must choose an angle and range to plot a course to get as close as possible to a black hole, and one where they select different astral bodies to see how they bend space-time around them.
<br/><br/><br/><br/><br/><br/>
![black hole](/img/hackathon_2019/Screenshot_2022-10-19_15-16-52_1920x1080.png "The black hole"){: .align-right}
The game contains several cinematic scenes, like the beginning where the player finds the spaceship, or the moment when the spaceship approaches the black hole.
