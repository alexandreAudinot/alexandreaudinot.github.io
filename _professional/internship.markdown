The goal of this internship was to explore the possibilities of a standard VR headset. The [company](https://www.virtamed.com) I worked for does medical simulators using realistic plastic body parts models, and wanted to try using a VR setup to make something that really uses the potential of VR. The idea they came up with was to srink the user down by a factor of 500 and explore the inside of a knee.

When putting the headset on, the user starts in a reproduction of the company's demoroom. A green screen recording of one of the company's CEO would then greet the user and tell them to walk to the simulator, which starts the game.

![application](/img/internship/3.png "The demoroom of the company as it appeared in the application"){: .align-right}
In the game, the user would first get to explore the environnement freely, learning how to use the controllers to teleport around, get info of elements inside the knee by pointing at them, or even bending the knee. When ready, they would have to find and collect 4 golden stars (representing loose bodies found in a real knee) hidden in the level in a limited time. When done, they would simply go back to the demoroom to receive congratulations.

This is the project that taught me the fundamentals of making a 3D scene look good : making materials, using normal maps, baking lights, writing shader for some effects. It was also my first experience making a multiplayer game, as at some point we made a prototype for a multi-users application where one user would be in the knee, and the other outside.
